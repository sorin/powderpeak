Privacy Policy

Effective Date: April 30, 2026  ·  Last Updated: April 30, 2026

Summary: We collect only the information needed to manage your reservation and support your stay. We do not sell your personal information or share your mobile number with third parties for marketing purposes.

1. Who We Are

PowderPeak ("we," "us," or "our") is a privately owned short-term vacation rental located in the Northstar-at-Tahoe community in Truckee, California. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your personal information when you book or stay at our property, or communicate with us.

For questions about this policy, contact us by text or call at (530) 580-4512.

2. Information We Collect

We may collect the following information from guests:

  • Full name and contact information (phone number, email address)
  • Booking details including reservation dates, number of guests, and check-in/check-out times
  • Payment information (processed by your booking platform — we do not store payment card data directly)
  • Communication records including text messages and voicemails sent to our guest support line
  • Maintenance or issue reports submitted during your stay

We collect this information when you book through a third-party platform (such as Airbnb or VRBO), contact us directly by phone or text, or communicate with us before, during, or after your stay.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use your information only for the following purposes:

  • To confirm and manage your reservation
  • To send check-in instructions, door access codes, and arrival information
  • To provide guest support and respond to maintenance or safety issues during your stay
  • To send check-out reminders and post-stay follow-up
  • To comply with legal obligations, including Placer County short-term rental permit requirements

We do not use your information for marketing or advertising purposes unrelated to your active reservation.

5. How We Share Your Information

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information. We may share limited information in the following circumstances only:

  • Service providers: We use Twilio to power our SMS guest communications. Twilio processes messages on our behalf and is bound by its own privacy policy.
  • Booking platforms: If you booked through Airbnb, VRBO, or a similar platform, your information is also subject to that platform's privacy policy.
  • Legal compliance: We may disclose information if required by law, such as to comply with Placer County short-term rental regulations or a valid legal request.
  • Safety: We may share information to protect the safety of guests, neighbors, or the property.

Mobile information (phone numbers collected for SMS communications) is not shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes.

6. Data Retention

We retain guest information for up to 3 years after your stay, or as required by applicable law. Communication records related to safety or legal matters may be retained longer. You may request deletion of your information by contacting us directly.

7. Your Rights

You have the right to request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal information. To exercise these rights, contact us by text at (530) 580-4512 or by email at powderpeaknorthstar@gmail.com. California residents may have additional rights under the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA).

8. Security

We take reasonable steps to protect your information from unauthorized access, use, or disclosure. Guest communications and booking data are handled through secure, reputable third-party platforms. However, no method of transmission over the Internet or electronic storage is completely secure.

9. Children's Privacy

Our rental is not marketed to or intended for children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date. Continued use of our guest services after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
