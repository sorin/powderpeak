Privacy Policy

Effective Date: May 5, 2026  ·  Last Updated: May 5, 2026

PowderPeak ("we," "us," or "our") is a privately owned short-term vacation rental located in the Northstar-at-Tahoe community in Truckee, California. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your personal information when you book or stay at our property, or communicate with us.

Information We Collect

We may collect the following types of personal information:

  • Contact information: Name, email address, and phone number provided when making a booking or contacting us.
  • Booking information: Reservation dates, number of guests, and special requests.
  • Communications: Text messages, emails, and other communications you send us.
  • Device and usage data: Basic information about how you access our website (browser type, IP address), collected through standard web hosting logs.

SMS Messaging Disclosures

By providing your phone number as part of the booking process or through our website contact form, you consent to receive text messages from PowderPeak regarding your reservation. This includes:

  • Booking confirmations and updates
  • Check-in instructions and door access codes
  • Property notices relevant to your stay
  • Guest support responses during your stay

How We Use Your Information

  • To fulfill your reservation and provide guest support during your stay.
  • To send reservation-related text messages and communications you have consented to receive.
  • To respond to inquiries and resolve issues.
  • To comply with legal obligations including Placer County short-term rental regulations (STR Permit A-9826).

How We Share Your Information

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information with third parties for marketing purposes. We may share information with:

  • Booking platforms (such as Airbnb or Vrbo) as part of managing your reservation through their services.
  • Service providers necessary to operate communications (such as our SMS service provider), solely for the purpose of providing the service.
  • Legal authorities if required by law, court order, or to protect our rights and safety.

Data Retention

We retain personal information for as long as necessary to fulfill your reservation, comply with legal obligations, and resolve disputes. SMS opt-out records are retained indefinitely to honor opt-out requests.

Your Rights

You have the right to request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal information. To exercise these rights, contact us by text at (530) 580-4512 or by email at powderpeaknorthstar@gmail.com. California residents may have additional rights under the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA).

Children's Privacy

Our services are not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The effective date at the top of this page will reflect the most recent update. Continued use of our services after changes constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.
